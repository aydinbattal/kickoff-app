# Privacy Policy - Kickoff

*Last updated: August 17, 2026*

**Kickoff** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Account & Profile Information
When you register for an account you are using [Supabase Authentication](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth). We collect your email address and authentication credentials. You may also provide profile information such as your display name and preferred soccer playing positions (Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, Forward).

### B. Session & Squad Data
We store the match sessions you create or join, including match date, kickoff time, pitch location, RSVP status, submitted squad formations, and post-match MVP votes.

### C. In-App Subscriptions & Purchases
If you purchase **Kickoff Premium**, transactions are processed directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store via RevenueCat. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card numbers or banking information.

### D. Advertising (Free Tier Users)
For users on the free tier, we use Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA) to deliver and measure ad performance in compliance with platform guidelines. AdMob is completely disabled for Kickoff Premium subscribers.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:
- Authenticate your identity and manage your account.
- Enable session creation, attendance management, and invite sharing.
- Execute our squad merge algorithm and MVP voting features.
- Validate and deliver Kickoff Premium subscription benefits.
- Monitor app performance and resolve technical errors.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

All network communications between the Kickoff mobile application and our backend services are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS protocols. Data is securely stored using Supabase PostgreSQL databases with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

---

## 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting our support team at [abattal35@gmail.com](mailto:abattal35@gmail.com). Upon account deletion, your personal profile information will be removed from our active databases.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Kickoff does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove the information.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
