# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Kickoff

### 1. How does the Squad Merge Engine work?
Kickoff allows any player attending the game to submit a tactical squad lineup. When the organizer triggers the merge, Kickoff's deterministic algorithm processes all submitted suggestions alongside player position preferences (Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, Forward) to produce a fair, balanced, and competitive team split (Team A vs Team B).

### 2. What match formats does Kickoff support?
Kickoff supports all common game formats from small-sided up to full pitch: **5v5**, **6v6**, **7v7**, **8v8**, **9v9**, **10v10**, and full **11v11** matches.

### 3. How does MVP voting work?
After a match is marked completed, attending players can cast a vote for the match's standout performer. Once voting closes, Kickoff calculates and displays the crowned **Match MVP**.

### 4. What is the difference between Free and Kickoff Premium?
- **Free Tier**: Join unlimited sessions, host 1 active session at a time, supported by standard banner and interstitial ads, with a 7-day session retention.
- **Kickoff Premium ($2.99/mo)**: Host up to 3 active concurrent sessions at once, completely ad-free, with permanent session history retention.

### 5. How do I invite players to my game session?
When you create a session, tap the **Share / Invite** button to copy a direct link or send it straight to your team's WhatsApp group, Discord channel, iMessage, or SMS thread.

### 6. Is Kickoff responsible for game injuries, field permits, or player disputes?
**No.** Kickoff is strictly a scheduling and coordination tool. Players voluntarily participate in pickup soccer at their own risk. Organizers and attendees are solely responsible for player safety, field permits, personal equipment, and respectful sportsmanship. Please review our [Terms of Use](terms/index.md) for full legal terms.

### 7. How do I manage or cancel my Kickoff Premium subscription?
- **iOS**: Open the App Store > tap your profile icon > **Subscriptions** > select **Kickoff** > tap **Cancel Subscription**.
- **Android**: Open the Google Play Store > tap your profile icon > **Payments & subscriptions** > **Subscriptions** > select **Kickoff** > tap **Cancel Subscription**.
