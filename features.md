# Features Overview - Kickoff

**Kickoff** is built specifically for soccer enthusiasts who want a reliable, modern, and stress-free way to organize pickup soccer games, build balanced teams, and celebrate the sport with their community.

---

## 1. Match & Session Management

- **Quick Game Setup**: Create a session in seconds with date, time, pitch address/name, player limit, and game format.
- **Instant Invites**: Generate shareable session links that can be sent directly to WhatsApp, iMessage, Discord, or SMS.
- **Real-Time Attendance & RSVPs**: Track confirmed attendees, available slots, and waitlists dynamically.
- **Match Status Lifecycle**: Seamlessly transition games from upcoming to active, completed, and archived.

---

## 2. Flexible Tactical Squad Builder (5v5 to 11v11)

- **Comprehensive Match Formats**:
  - 5v5 Futsal / Small-sided
  - 6v6 & 7v7 Turf / Half-pitch
  - 8v8, 9v9 & 10v10 Intermediate / Large-sided formats
  - 11v11 Full-pitch standard soccer
- **Tactical Pitch Board**: Visual field layout with dedicated starting positions for Team A and Team B.
- **Crowd-Sourced Suggestions**: Allow players in the session to submit their own preferred squad lineups before kickoff.

---

## 3. Smart Squad Merge Engine

- **Deterministic Balancing Algorithm**: Combines submitted squad suggestions into an optimized, fair final team matchup.
- **Position Harmony**: Integrates each player's registered preferred position (GK, DEF, MID, FWD) to prevent lopsided team setups (e.g., all defenders on one side).
- **Zero Arguments on Game Day**: Neutral, algorithmic team allocation eliminates disputes over team fairness.

---

## 4. Deciding Match MVP

- **Post-Game Voting**: Once the referee blows the final whistle, session attendees can cast their vote for the matchday MVP.
- **Player Recognition**: Crown the MVP of the match and celebrate standout goals, defensive stops, and game-winning plays.

---

## 5. Player Profiles & Preferred Positions

- **Custom Profiles**: Set your player name and multi-select preferred positions (Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, Forward).
- **Match History**: Keep track of matches played and team rosters over time.

---

## 6. Kickoff Premium

- **Multi-Session Hosting**: Host and manage up to 3 active concurrent game sessions at once.
- **100% Ad-Free**: Enjoy an uninterrupted experience with zero banner or interstitial ads.
- **Permanent Session Archive**: Retain all your historical game logs, squad formations, and MVP awards permanently.
